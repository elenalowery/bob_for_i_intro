Quality of Agentic Output

What drives output quality — and what you can control

Output quality depends on

  • Your prompt You control
  • Context — provided by you and retained in memory Mostly controllable
  • The quality of the LLM Vendor-determined

What's included in the context?

  • All inputs and outputs of the current chat session
  • Files and folders explicitly referenced in your prompt
  • The entire open workspace project — unless you instruct Bob to ignore it
  • Matched modes and skills — targeted instructions Bob applies based on the task
